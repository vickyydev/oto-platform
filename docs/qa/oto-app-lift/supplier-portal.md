The supplier report-detail route used the wrapper returned by storage as though it were the report, so valid magic links received 403. The portal now checks the nested report's tenant and branch, returns the flat report shape expected by its page, and maps its media to a token-scoped read route. Report detail, comments, close and media reads also require the token's tenant and branch, and media must be listed on that report.
