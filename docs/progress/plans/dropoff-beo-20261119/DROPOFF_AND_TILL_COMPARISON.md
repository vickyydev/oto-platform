## 2. Drop-off today, step by step

The till's steps, as `pages/Till.tsx` numbers them: 1 Membership Check, 2 rate, 3 tickets, 4 details (rarely shown), 5 payment, 6 done, 7 the drop-off or nanny form, 8 the saved children review. The customer display is the paired display at the counter (`pages/Display.tsx`); the till's own customer half shows the same screens when no display is paired. Which child needs drop-off, a nanny or nothing is decided by the park's supervision rules from the child's age (set in the Console).

### (a) A first-time family at the counter

1. **Display: language.** Five languages, each in its own words. Till: "Membership Check, waiting for the customer".
2. **Display: phone number.** Required, with a line saying why (the child's safety), the messaging app (WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE), and a nickname, which is optional. Continue.
3. **Till: the number is looked up.** Not found: a "New member?" box on the till; staff type a name and press Create member, or Continue as walk-in. Display: a welcome screen.
4. **Till: rate.** Three cards (Tourist, Expat, Thai). Expat: a document check (kind of document, expiry date optional). Thai: one confirmation with two buttons.
5. **Till: tickets.** Staff pick the play ticket and set the number of kids, no adult. Display: the order as it is built.
6. **Till: Pay.** With no adult on the sale, the till opens "Children playing alone": one card per child with name, date of birth or age, and Waive where an older sibling may cover. The family's form opens on the display at the same time.
7. **Display: the drop-off or nanny form, three steps.** (i) Children: each child's name and age, what each one needs and its price (drop-off fee, or a nanny's hourly rate). (ii) Care notes: allergies and medical, food notes. (iii) Agreements: the parent or guardian name, the number filled in from step 2 (can be changed) with its app and a tick "this is my number for this app", a photo of each child with the guardian taken on the display's camera, the consent tick, and the park's confirmations. Done. Till: a read-only copy of what the family types, beside the staff card; staff can help in "Private child details" (every field except the ticks; the food choice for the child is only here).
8. **When the family presses Done with everything complete, the till writes it all.** The member (created now from the form's number and parent name if not created in step 3), each child saved to that member, the visit, the registration with the consent and the ticks, and any waiver. A nanny child without a nanny then goes to the nanny picker (only nannies on shift at this park); otherwise payment.
9. **Payment.** The display shows the whole order and the amount. Hold sale and Save as partly paid are there.
10. **After payment: "Check in now" or "Leave as booked".** Check in now prints the child's band with the supervision badge and the allergy line, starts the timer and loads any prepaid food credit. Receipt.
11. **The Check-in board** holds the family with a countdown; staff can edit a child's details and the pickup list there.
12. **Pickup** (`components/dropoff/CheckOutModal.tsx`): pick the collector from the list (or add one on the spot with a photo), compare with the drop-off photo and take a pickup photo, see the prepaid food summary (refunded or forfeited by the park's setting), release.

### (b) A returning family at the counter

Steps 1 and 2 are the same. Then:

3. **Till: found.** The member card, the rate set from their verified rate, and the "visiting children" window: every saved child ticked, every field editable (allergies, medical alert and notes, food notes), Confirm writes a visit. Display: "Welcome back".
4. **Rate:** only their own card when their rate is verified and in date (one press).
5. **Tickets:** staff set the kids count again by hand.
6. **Pay opens step 8 first, the saved children review.** Till and display show one place per child counted on the ticket, filled from the saved list in list order (not from the children ticked in step 3). On the display the family confirms "Still correct?" for each, may change a name or age, pick another saved child, remove a saved child, or add a new child (ages 0 to 12). Care notes are not shown on the display. Done.
7. **The form (step 7)** follows with names and ages filled in, care notes hidden for saved children, the parent name empty, the number filled with its tick to give again, the photo to take again, and the consent and confirmations to tick again. Then as steps 8 to 12 of (a).

### (c) What a parent can do on their own phone today

- **The booking site** (`/book`, `pages/Book.tsx`): the phone number; a code by SMS for a returning family, which loads the saved children (changes are saved to the family under SCRUM-1011 Online booking: a returning family's changes are saved to their family record, Testing); tickets; the saved children; the same drop-off form (`ConsentCapture`, with the photo left for reception); then payment online only, by card or PromptPay through 2C2P.
- Only once paid is the registration written; the children wait on the Check-in board. At the park the booking's QR is redeemed at the till or the kiosk, and the children are checked in from the board with nothing charged again.
- There is no drop-off-only form, no QR code that opens one, and no "pay at reception".

### Where things are asked more than once in one visit

- **The phone number:** at step 2, and again on the drop-off and nanny form (filled in, to be confirmed with a tick; the owner's ruling of 12 November). It is not asked again before payment.
- **The parent's name:** the optional nickname at step 2, the name staff type in "New member?", and the parent or guardian name on the form, which starts empty every visit, even for a member.
- **The children (returning family):** ticked by staff at step 3, counted by staff on the ticket at step 5, confirmed one by one by the family at step 8, and shown again with names and ages on the form.
- **Every visit:** the photo of each child with the guardian, the number tick, the consent and the confirmations.
