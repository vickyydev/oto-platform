## Form-builder staging publication gate

Before a further public drop-off form-builder walkthrough on live `b20ed05b`, route review found that the admin form and public `GET /api/public/dropoff-form/:branchId` both select the tenant-wide form with `branch_id IS NULL`. Publishing a changed version would therefore replace the public form for every staging branch in that tenant, including any live bench-testing flow. The publish route also queues a translation job and synchronously calls the translation service for Thai, Russian, and Chinese; there is no branch-specific public form selection or version rollback endpoint. Public drop-off submission itself writes check-in rows and photo/signature objects and does not call an SMS, email, or LINE sender in that handler, but would show a new child on the staff board.

No form, check-in, media, or account fixture was created for this follow-up. The requested changed-form publish and real public result screenshots remain unverified until the walkthrough can use an isolated tenant/form or a controlled tenant-wide staging change with an exact restore path.
