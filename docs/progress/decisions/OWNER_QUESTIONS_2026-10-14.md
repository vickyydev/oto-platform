# OTO Park — questions for the owner

14 October 2026

These are the open business questions on the new system. Each one explains
why the answer matters for how the system behaves. Reply with the number
and your answer.

---

## A. Money rules

**A1. A party's money on the daily report.** When a family pays today for a
party happening next month, should today's revenue include that payment, or
should the money count on the party's date?
*Why it matters: this defines what "today's party revenue" means on the
daily dashboard, and the party report cannot be finished without it.*

**A2. Who may issue credit vouchers from the till.** The till can issue
vouchers that add spendable credit nobody paid for. Should these be limited
to managers only, or may any reception staff issue them? Either way they
appear under promotions in the reports, so given-away credit stays visible.
*Why it matters: this creates stored value without payment, so the till
needs a clear rule about who is allowed to create it.*

**A3. Credit spent at the other branch.** When credit bought at one park is
spent at the other, the records already connect the two sides: the selling
park keeps the money and the liability, and the spending park's report
shows the redemption as its own line, so the link between the two branches'
records is visible and network totals add up. Is anything extra needed on
either branch's records beyond this?
*Why it matters: if the branches settle this money between themselves in a
particular way, the reports should show the figures that settlement needs.*

**A4. Money during an internet outage.** Two linked rules to set:
(a) if the card terminal took the payment but the till never received the
answer, may staff type in the terminal's approval code so the sale is
captured and reconciled later?
(b) what daily spending cap should apply to wristband credit while offline
(currently ฿300 per wristband per day), and should same-day credit expire
that night?
*Why it matters: these two rules decide what money can move while the
internet is down, and what the morning reconciliation has to match up.*

**A5. Different VAT by product category.** Does any branch ever need a
different tax treatment per category — for example food taxed differently
from tickets? Today every branch uses one tax rule, and an unused
category-exception option sits in the system.
*Why it matters: if this is ever needed, the option gets a proper
administration screen; if never, the unused option is retired.*

## B. What staff may do

Answer each with "yes", "no", or your own rule.

**B1. Refunds.** Today a refund needs a manager's approval. Should
reception be able to refund a sale without a manager?
*Why it matters: decides whether the desk can help a family immediately or
must call a manager over.*

**B2. Refund after the card window closes.** When a card payment can no
longer be cancelled on the terminal, the refund is paid in cash today.
Should it instead go back to the family's card where the terminal supports
that? (What the park's terminals actually support needs confirming with the
payment provider.)
*Why it matters: affects the cash drawer's reconciliation and how long the
family waits for their money.*

**B3. Marking a whole order free.** A manager can make an entire order free
of charge — a "full comp", used for example after a complaint or for a
guest of the park. Today the manager's own recorded action is all that is
required. Should a second person have to approve it first?
*Why it matters: a full comp is money given away; the question is whether
one manager's word is enough.*

**B4. A cashier's own staff purchase.** May a cashier ring up their own
staff-benefit purchase at the till they are operating (both identities are
recorded on the sale), or must another staff member serve them?
*Why it matters: decides whether a staff purchase needs a second employee
present.*

**B5. Discounts during an outage.** While the internet is down, discounts
and comps keep the same permissions as online, and refunds stay impossible
offline. Should any discount or comp need a second person specifically
during an outage?
*Why it matters: offline actions are checked after the fact rather than
live, so this is the window where a stricter rule could apply.*

**B6. Removing a member's verified rate.** Removing a member's verified
local-resident rate from their saved record is manager-only today. Is that
right, or may reception do it?
*Why it matters: separates a one-sale price choice from permanently
changing what the family's record says.*

**B7. Who opens the Radar revenue dashboard.** Today: administrators only.
Should branch managers have it too? Note: Radar shows every park's figures,
so a manager of one branch would see all branches.
*Why it matters: decides who can see cross-park revenue.*

**B8. Staff benefit numbers and where they spend.** The configured amounts
today: Owner — everything free; Manager — 2 coffees/day, ฿500/month
allowance, 30% off food; Staff — 2 coffees/day, 30% off food. Benefits are
currently spendable at food counters only. Are these the right amounts, and
should benefits also be accepted at the ticket till?
*Why it matters: these numbers are live rules at the till; they stay
editable at any time.*

## C. Customers and families

**C1. Party extras and the kitchen.** Should party add-ons (extra pizzas,
socks) create normal kitchen tickets, stock movements and sales records
like any other order, or stay only on the party bill?
*Why it matters: decides whether the kitchen automatically sees the order
and whether stock counts stay true without extra typing.*

**C2. Old families in the new member list.** When the old system's data is
brought over, should past check-in families automatically become member
records (marked unverified), giving reception a full member list from day
one — or should the member list start only with families who explicitly
registered?
*Why it matters: decides what reception sees when they search a returning
family's phone number after the switch.*

---

## D. One boundary that touches everything

**D1. When the business day starts.** Reports, prices, promotions and daily
staff benefits all currently reset at 05:00 Bangkok time rather than
midnight — late trading stays with the same day's cash-up, and a promotion
does not flip at midnight in the middle of an evening. Confirm 05:00 as the
single boundary for all of these, or choose a different rule.
*Why it matters: this one boundary defines which day every sale, price and
benefit belongs to.*
