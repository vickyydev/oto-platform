What was wrong: a child whose drop-off was already paid for at the till could still be offered "Add to sale" in the Add drop-off child picker and be charged a second time, most of all when the till was reloaded or nobody chose anything after the payment.

What a person sees now (checked on staging at commit fe6181bd, ZZ TEST records and cash only): in the Add drop-off child picker a child paid at the till reads "Paid at till · Z740-000001", the same receipt number as on the receipt, the family button reads "Paid at till" and is greyed out, and there is no Add to sale (Add sibling still works). This holds after "Leave as booked" and also when the till was reloaded before any choice. If a second sale for that child is still sent, it is refused before any money is taken ("already paid for at the till on receipt Z740-000002, so there is nothing to charge ... Nothing was saved"), and the Console lists the refusal under Activity as {{checkin.second_charge_refused}} with the paying receipt. On the Check-in board the family reads "Booked & paid · no payment needed", and Check in at the board takes no money: it goes straight to "Check in & issue band" with no payment screen. Families who paid online still read "Paid online". The counter box and the booth get the same change with the next box release.

How to retest on staging: sign in to a till as reception, ring up a walk-in with two kids and no adult on a play ticket, fill the drop-off form on the customer screen, pay in cash and choose "Leave as booked". Then press "Skip — walk-in", pick a tier and press "Add drop-off child": both children read "Paid at till · <receipt number>" with no Add to sale. Open Check-in and find the family: it reads "Booked & paid · no payment needed"; press Check in and no payment screen opens.
