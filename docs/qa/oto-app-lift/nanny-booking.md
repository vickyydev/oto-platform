# Nanny assignment and reservations — local verification

The app now requires an active employee with a nanny role, a same-branch clock-in and no unavailable marker before assigning care. The picker excludes nannies with a current reservation. Reservations validate their branch, optional linked check-in, date and same-day time window. Booking and service edits serialize by nanny/date; a conflicting edit leaves the previous reservation intact. Reservation activation requires the nanny to be on duty, and completed or cancelled bookings cannot be reopened through the status route.

Two simultaneous identical bookings returned 201 and 409 with exactly one booking stored. A conflicting service edit returned 409 without cancelling its original booking; a non-conflicting replacement returned 200 with one cancelled and one reserved row. Invalid status returned 400, off-duty and wrong-branch activation 409, activation/completion 200, reopening completed 409. All probe accounts, devices, sessions, employees, check-ins and reservations were removed; residual fixture counts were zero. No new test suite was committed.
