# Lucky Wheel vouchers at the counter

For reception and restaurant staff. A family brings a slip from the Lucky Wheel booth. It shows the prize, a code of 11 letters and digits that starts with the booth's own two characters (`B2…` for a booth whose prefix is B2), the same code as a QR, the booth, the name of whoever was signed in there ("Staff"), and when it expires. A slip is used **once**, **one per sale**, never together with a promo code, and only at a till that is **online**.

## 1. Which till

The ticket till is the POS's **Tickets** tab; the restaurant till is its **F&B** tab.

| The slip | Where it is redeemed |
|---|---|
| 100, 150 or 200 THB off · 1+1 Kids Ticket | The ticket till |
| A free item from the shop or the ticket add-ons, such as the Bracelet Workshop | The ticket till |
| A gift handed over as it is (a "hand-over prize") | The ticket till, at reception |
| Kids Pizza, or anything else from the restaurant menu | The restaurant (F&B) till. The ticket till answers "Redeem this voucher at the restaurant till" |

## 2. At the ticket till

1. Start the sale as usual: the member or a walk-in, and whatever else they are buying.
2. Type the code off the slip into **Redeem voucher**, under the promo code box, and press **Redeem** — or scan the slip's QR with the counter's scanner, which does the same. Spaces, dashes and small letters do not matter. After its first two characters a code never has a 0, 1, I, L, O or U in it.
3. The **voucher card** appears, in green. Check it against the slip: the prize, the code, the booth and when it was printed, the last day it is valid, and "Printed by" — the name on the slip's Staff line. (The card writes dates its own way — "9 Oct 2026" where the slip has "09 Oct 2026", and "Sep" where the slip has "Sept" — but they are the same dates.)
4. Do what the card says (section 4).
5. **Pay** → the *Customer Details* screen (the family's display asks for their details; the till waits until they finish or skip) → how the family pays → **Confirm Payment Received**. When the sale comes to ฿0 there is nothing to choose: the screen says "No payment needed" — press **Complete Sale**. The voucher is used up only now; the confirmation says "Voucher … used".

## 3. At the restaurant till

1. **No wristband — continue as guest**, or scan the family's band.
2. Type the code into **Redeem voucher** and press **Redeem**, or scan the slip. (Typed or scanned on the first screen, the code opens a guest order with the voucher on it.)
3. The card appears, and the item goes on the order at ฿0, marked "Voucher".
4. Add anything else they order, then **Charge** as usual. With nothing else on the order it is **Charge ฿0**: after the pick-up code the screen says "No payment needed" — press **Complete Order**. The confirmation says "Voucher … used".

## 4. What the card says, and what you do

| The card says | What you do |
|---|---|
| "150 THB off the ticket order" (or 100, or 200) | Put the tickets on the sale first. The amount comes off the tickets, never more than they cost; no change is given. |
| "Second kids ticket free — 1 Hour Play" (the package it names) | Put two kids tickets of that package on the sale; one kid's ticket comes off. |
| "Ring up to use it, then hand over: Bracelet Workshop" (a free item) | The item is on the sale at ฿0. Hand it over once the sale is confirmed: the confirmation says "Voucher … used · Hand over: …". |
| "Ring up to use it, then hand over: …" (a gift) | **Pay** works with nothing else on the sale: a ฿0 sale, "No payment needed" → **Complete Sale**. Hand the gift over once the confirmation says "Hand over: …". |

Kids Pizza is the pizza only; a drink with it is paid for as usual.

**Taking it off.** Before Pay, the bin on the card takes the voucher off the sale. After Pay it stays with that sale until the sale is paid or voided: **Cancel** (at the restaurant till, **Back**, then **Clear**) voids a sale that took no money, and the voucher is free again.

## 5. When the till says no

| The till says | What it means, and what to do |
|---|---|
| Invalid code | A typing mistake. Check the code against the slip. |
| Code not found — the booth may not have synced yet | The slip was printed while the booth had no internet and has not reached the till yet. See section 7 — do not keep pressing Redeem. |
| Already redeemed on … at … by … | It was used then, there, by that person. Hand nothing over. |
| Voucher expired on … | Its last day has passed. |
| This voucher is in use at … | Another till has it on an order it has not rung up. That till can take it off; if it does not, another till can take it over once it has been held there for 15 minutes. |
| This voucher is on a sale already rung up at … — pay or void that sale first | See section 6. |
| Redeem this voucher at the restaurant till | A menu item: the restaurant till takes it. |
| This voucher's item is not sold at this branch — ask a manager | Its free item, or its 1+1's ticket package, is not sold at this park. |
| This voucher's item is not set up yet — ask a manager | Its voucher type has no product or ticket package linked yet. |
| This voucher has been cancelled | It cannot be used. |
| Only one voucher can be used on a sale · A voucher cannot be combined with another voucher or promo code on the same sale | One voucher per sale, never with a promo code. |
| "…" is a promo code — enter it in the promo code box | It is one of the park's own discount codes, not a slip. |
| This voucher comes off tickets, and this sale has no tickets left to take it off | Put the tickets on the sale. |
| The 1+1 kids ticket needs two kids tickets of … on this sale | Put two kids tickets of that package on the sale. |
| This sale has already been rung up — scan the voucher before Pay | Put the voucher on the sale before pressing Pay. |
| This sale is finished — start a new sale to redeem the voucher | Start a new sale, then scan it. |
| Scan the voucher at this till first — it is not held for this sale | Type or scan the code again. |
| The platform is still pricing the voucher — press again in a moment | Wait a second and press again. |
| Vouchers can only be redeemed online | This till is working offline. See section 7. |
| Too many wrong codes — try again in … minutes | See section 7. |

## 6. A sale rung up and left unpaid

Pay puts the voucher on that sale. If the payment screen is then left without finishing or cancelling the sale — the till locks after two minutes untouched, another tab is pressed, or the page reloads — the sale stays unpaid and keeps the voucher, and the slip then answers "This voucher is on a sale already rung up at … — pay or void that sale first".

- **At the till named in that answer**, the answer also shows that unpaid sale — "Unpaid sale at …", when it was rung up and for how much — and a button, **Void that unpaid sale and use the voucher here**. If the sale was rung up less than 15 minutes ago, it warns that it may still be being paid for on another screen: first make sure nobody at that till is taking money for it. Then press the button. The old sale is voided, with the reason recorded, and the voucher goes on the sale in front of you. Without the till's void permission there is no button, and the answer says so.
- **Or in History**, at any till: open the sale (it shows as unpaid) → **Void unpaid sale** → a reason → **Void this sale**. It warns the same way about a sale rung up less than 15 minutes ago.

Only a sale that took no money can be voided.

## 7. Wrong codes, slips printed offline, and a till offline

- **Five wrong codes inside a minute** — mistyped, or not found — lock voucher redemption at that till for **10 minutes**, for every family, and put a warning in the Console for the managers ("Somebody may be guessing codes"). Nobody can lift the lock sooner; another till still works. Five wrong codes from one person inside a minute, at any tills, also lock that person out of voucher redemption at every till for **10 minutes** — the till says "Too many wrong codes from you — you cannot redeem vouchers at any till for … minutes" — while everybody else can still redeem. If the till is locked as well, it shows whichever of the two locks lasts longer.
- **A slip printed while the booth had no internet** answers "Code not found — the booth may not have synced yet" until the booth is back online and has sent it — usually within a minute of its internet coming back. **Press Redeem once.** If that is the answer, trying the same slip again does no harm — one code counts as one wrong code, however often it is tried — but it works only after the booth has sent it. Try once more later — when the dot in the corner of the booth's television is green again — or at another till.
- **The till must be online**: a till working offline answers "Vouchers can only be redeemed online".
