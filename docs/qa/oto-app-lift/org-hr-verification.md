## Org Chart and Activity Log read-only baseline, 1 October

The Org Chart reporting-line walkthrough was paused before creating employees or changing nodes. With the existing launcher-signed administrator on live source `b20ed05b`, `/api/org-chart/budget` returned 500 with `Cannot convert undefined or null to object`. Source inspection points to `server/orgChartRoutes.ts` budget `getNodesForMode()`: it selects `employees.baseSalaryMonthly`, but `baseSalaryMonthly` is defined on `employeePayrollProfiles`, not `employees`. This is the likely cause of the Drizzle selection failure. The safe app-owned repair is to join the payroll profile by employee and signed-in tenant, select its nullable salary, and convert the numeric value when summing. `GET /api/org-chart/nodes` was deliberately not called: that handler auto-inserts missing active employees as chart nodes on read, so using it before the budget repair could change unrelated staff chart state. No organization fixture was created and no reporting relationship was claimed as tested.

The Activity Logbook was checked separately without writes. `/api/activity-logs?search=ZZ%20SCRUM193%20ORG&limit=20` returned 200 with zero matches; `/api/activity-logs/summary?sinceDays=30` returned 200. The real 820 × 1180 staging screenshot `scrum-193-staging-activity-logbook-baseline.png` was visually reviewed and shows the filtered empty-state screen. It proves the page and filter render, not that a reporting-line change appears in history. The Org Chart create/edit handlers do not write activity records.

Source review also found an open tenant boundary: `activity_log` has no `tenant_id`; the Logbook list/count and summary storage queries have no tenant predicate, and an administrator with no branch filter may read shared rows. Company-wide rows with no branch cannot be classified reliably by a branch-only filter. A platform-lane additive tenant column and legacy-row cutover, followed by app route scoping, are needed before cross-tenant Activity Log acceptance. Restricted role and branch denial remain unverified on staging.
