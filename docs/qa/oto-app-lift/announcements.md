# SCRUM-193: Announcements staging acceptance

On live `oto-app-staging` deployment `dep-daupqp60tbcc73c7ehv0` (`858df417`), a synthetic manager signed in through the launcher to a disposable second tenant. The manager had access to one of that tenant's two branches. Publishing an announcement targeted to the allowed branch returned 200; `/api/announcements/active?branchId=…` returned 200 and included it. The [announcements-targeted-tablet-2026-10-01.png](announcements-targeted-tablet-2026-10-01.png) screenshot shows the live announcement, its one-branch audience, and the signed-in branch at a real 820 × 1180 viewport. Visual review found its text and controls readable without blocking layout.

The park-tenant administrator's announcement list returned 200 without the synthetic announcement, and its attempt to read the second tenant's branch feed returned 404. The restricted manager received 403 when targeting the park tenant's branch, 403 when publishing to its own tenant's other branch, and 403 when reading that other branch's active feed. These are API refusal checks from two independent launcher-authenticated browser contexts; the screenshot shows the positive published state rather than a refusal page.

The announcement was deleted through its tenant's session (200). The synthetic task, two branches, app user and branch-access row, and tenant were deleted; the platform account was unlinked and deactivated. No active synthetic tenant or announcement remains. This is targeted publish/read and tenant/branch isolation evidence, not an exhaustive announcement scheduling or notification regression.
