# SCRUM-726 measurement plan

Plan before any measurement or load: I will use the new staging POS address only, in the next quiet STATUS proof window after 502, 503 and 673. The assumed normal park is six tills, six customer displays and two check-in boards, with two wristband lookups per till per minute and one cash sale per till every two minutes. Those device counts and arrival rates are estimates, not measured park demand. This tests cloud traffic through the forwarder; it does not claim Pi, iPad or offline capacity.

I will first check current users, exact live commits and green CI, then measure the background on its own. I will measure each idle case separately for at least three minutes after warm-up: one till, one display, the pair, and one check-in board. The existing two-minute staff lock stays unchanged: the signed-in idle measurements use harmless browser activity with no orders, and I will also let a till lock naturally and report its locked cost separately. Real browser traffic and read-only PostgreSQL row-change counters will give requests and database writes per minute. Background activity will be shown separately, with table-level deltas and the limits of subtracting a shared database baseline.

For the busy steps I will use real till/display/board browsers and replay the same authenticated routes for additional stations. I will step through 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12 and, only while the safety lines hold, 24 and 48 tills with matching displays and one board per three tills. Each step lasts at least three minutes; no jump exceeds twice the previous count. If latency starts to rise sharply I will stop increasing and report that bend. I will keep all writes single-attempt, with stable sale IDs and Idempotency-Key, and check exactly one finalised sale and one approved cash attempt for each attempted cash sale in the database after every step. A mismatched count stops the test immediately.

Stop lines: stop all generated busy traffic immediately on an unexpected write response, any sale-count mismatch, readiness failure, a lost lease or evidence that a real person's staging work could be affected. Also stop on more than 1% unexpected failed calls in any 60-second window, read-call p95 above the larger of 1 second or twice the warm baseline in two consecutive windows, or CPU or memory at 80% of its limit or database connections at 60% of their limit. An unexpected 5xx, timeout or a new non-test sale pauses the ramp for investigation before another step. I will not deliberately drive staging to failure.

After recovery I will run at least two continuous hours at the lowest representative mix: one native till/display pair, one native check-in board, two lookups per minute and one cash sale every two minutes. I will record per-route p50/p95, errors, open streams, lease renewal, and forwarder/API/database CPU and memory throughout, plus database connections and row changes. Every step and the soak get independent sale counts. The soak must show stable memory after warm-up, no accumulating streams and continuing leases. The report will separate measured limits from estimates and will recommend production sizing without changing any Render size or plan.

The only planned API setting is temporary BOX_AGENT_TEST_SLOT for one dedicated ZZ TEST virtual box, if a running hosted test box is needed. I will read every current value privately, stop if the key is already owned by another proof, add that one key through its single-key endpoint, confirm the name count rises by one and all other values remain byte-identical, then restart the same source. Cleanup removes only that key with the same checks and same-source restart. A new ZZ TEST menu item with no stock links will keep generated sales away from park stock. No new Render service, address move, old-site setting change or production action. I will retire the exact test menu item/category and stations/displays, release test leases, sign out the test sessions, and archive/disable the test box with readback. Cash audit rows stay labelled for traceability. No SMS, real person or park device is used.

Small wasted requests will be fixed only if the change is within this lane and preserves behavior, then remeasured with the required gates. Anything larger or owned by the sign-in/outage lane gets its own ticket. Deployed requires the actual numbers, cleanup, an opened proof image and the Jira helper walk; a safety stop or an incomplete soak will be stated plainly and will not be called Deployed.
