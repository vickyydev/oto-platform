## 1. What you asked for, as I understand it

1. You set up the prizes and their chances yourself, on the staging website.
2. You set up your Raspberry Pi as the booth box and connect it to the TV and the red button. One press gives one spin.
3. Every win prints a voucher on the receipt printer at the booth. Its QR belongs to that one win: it has an identity.
4. The park decides first what a QR is worth: a prize to hand over, or a discount. The identity comes "from the backend, or whatever is better", and you want a recommendation.
5. At the park counter, the handheld scanner reads the voucher, and the POS shows at once what to hand over or what to take off.
6. The POS puts that discount, or the prize's price, on the sale by itself. Nobody types anything.
7. The POS records the redemption, and the same QR can never be used again. This replaces today's static QRs, which anyone can reuse.
8. You test all of this on your bench against staging before anything goes to the mall. It comes first, ahead of the remaining defects and the rest of Sprint 2.

## 2. How the game works today on the platform, in plain words

**In short.**
- **The first half works on staging, in a web browser:** the button, the wheel, the prize and the voucher record.
- **Your Pi and your printer cannot act as the booth box yet.** On staging the "box" is a virtual one inside our server on Render, so no voucher reaches paper.
- **The second half has not been started:** the scan at the counter, the POS showing and applying the voucher, and the one-time use.

**The old game, for comparison** (B §0):
1. The TV's own browser picked the prize.
2. The TV showed a web-link QR. A parent's phone opened it and received a 4-digit code from Radar.
3. At reception, staff entered the 4 digits on Radar's `/redeem` page, typed or read by a camera. The first look-up already used the code up.
4. Staff then scanned a separate, **reusable** static QR into the old POS to apply the discount.

So there were two systems and two scans, and nothing linked them. Your 24 September request removes exactly that gap: one scan into the POS, which shows the voucher, applies it and uses it up.

## 4. The differences that matter, ranked

### 4.1 First: the counter, where one scan should show the voucher, apply it and use it up (NOT BUILT)

**What you asked for.** A scan shows at once what the voucher is. The POS loads the discount or the price. The voucher is recorded as redeemed and can never be used again.

**What exists [V].**
- **The identity already exists.** Every booth voucher has a unique record in the central database (`packages/db/src/schema/promo.ts:187-303`, filled by `apps/api/src/services/sync-booth.ts:357-800`). The record holds:
  - the prize;
  - the voucher type, which says what the voucher is worth;
  - the booth and the time;
  - the staff member on shift;
  - the expiry.
- **The six voucher types on staging** already say what each prize is: 100, 150 and 200 THB as an amount off; Bracelet Workshop and Kids Pizza as free items; 1+1 Kids Ticket as a free ticket. But the free items and the ticket point at no product or package, so a till would not know what to put on the bill (`booth-audit/api/console_200__api_voucher_definitions.json`; C-B14).
- **Nothing reads any of this at a till.** No route looks a voucher up or uses it up (§3 R16, R23 to R25).
- **Two traps for whoever builds it.**
  - The sale service accepts whatever discount the till describes (`apps/api/src/services/sale.ts:238-254`). A voucher's value must therefore be worked out on the server.
  - Voucher types default to "may be accepted offline" (`packages/db/src/schema/promo.ts:73-83`). Honouring that would break one-time use, and spec §8 says a printed slip is never trusted offline.

**What I propose to build.** This is SCRUM-207, best split into two or three tickets (§7).
1. **Look a voucher up without using it up.** A scan asks the backend what the voucher is and what state it is in, and consumes nothing. The answers use the plan's exact wording (`docs/progress/SPRINT_2_PLAN.md:1825-1831`):
   - "Code not found — the booth may not have synced yet";
   - "Invalid code";
   - "Already redeemed on <date time> at <branch/station> by <staff>";
   - "Voucher expired on <date>".
2. **Hold it at the scan and use it up at payment** (recommended; see Q2).
   - The till shows a card, for example "150 THB off your ticket order · Booth 1 · 24 Sept 15:02 · valid until 8 Oct", and puts the discount or the free item on the sale.
   - The voucher is then held for that sale. A scan at another till says the voucher is in use there.
   - When the sale is paid, one atomic update marks it redeemed with the sale, the staff member, the branch and the station.
   - Removing the line or voiding the sale releases it.
   - This matches the park's own wording: "After Reception scans and applies the prize, the voucher status becomes Redeemed and cannot be used again" (prize list L66) [V].
3. **The value comes from the voucher type, on the server**, never from the till.
4. **Booth vouchers are redeemed online only**, whatever the type's offline setting says (spec §8).
5. **The till listens for the scanner.**
   - The ticket till hears the handheld scanner the same way the shop screen has since S2-09 (`apps/pos/src/pages/MerchStation.tsx:299-353`; `apps/pos/src/lib/scanChannel.ts`), including the polling fallback that was proven on staging on 24 September.
   - So does the restaurant till, if Kids Pizza is redeemed there (Q8).
   - A scanner plugged straight into the computer running the POS types the code like a keyboard: the park's Zebra scanner sends the code and then Enter (`docs/architecture/DEVICE_INVENTORY.md:31`). The till must catch that input too, which the bench test needs.
   - A "Redeem voucher" box takes a typed code when a QR will not scan.
6. **The rules for each prize** (Q4), and the limit on guessing (5 misses a minute lock the station for 10 minutes, `SPRINT_2_PLAN.md:1832-1834`).
7. **A check character in the code**, so a mistyped code reads "Invalid code" rather than "not synced yet".
   - The plan's own S2-10b criterion, "a booth code with one altered character shows 'Invalid code'" (`SPRINT_2_PLAN.md:1848-1850`), cannot be met today, because the format has no check character (`packages/shared/src/booth-code.ts:44-80`) [V/I].
   - It should land before real vouchers are handed to customers.

### 4.2 Second: your Pi and your printer cannot act as the booth yet (NOT BUILT)

- **Only the virtual box exists.** On staging, the booth's box is a virtual one inside our server on Render (`apps/api/src/services/box.ts:1522-1579`). The server answers a TV only for a booth whose box runs in that same server. Any other booth gets `503 BOOTH_NOT_ON_THIS_BOX` (`apps/api/src/services/booth.ts:230-291`) [V].
- **The parts exist but are never assembled.** Each piece a Pi needs is built and tested on its own:
  - a local database (`packages/box-agent/src/store-sqlite.ts`);
  - a credential file (`packages/box-agent/src/credentials.ts:70`);
  - network printing (`packages/box-agent/src/printing/channel.ts:157-275`);
  - the booth's own request handler (`packages/box-agent/src/booth-http.ts:110-238`).

  Nothing puts them together into a program that runs on a Pi: there is no start script, no local web server, no image and no auto-start (C-B24; `packages/box-agent/package.json`) [V].
- **Two small changes to the TV page are also needed** [I]:
  - A page served by the Pi itself must not ask to be paired. The Pi's handler has no pairing route (`booth-http.ts:130-224`), yet the page always opens on "Pair this screen" when it holds no credential (`apps/booth/src/App.tsx:76-78`).
  - The page's server address is fixed when the page is built (`apps/booth/src/booth/client.ts:116-120`).
- **Printing works over the network (port 9100) only**, never by USB (§3 R19). A Pi 5 has one network socket. On the bench, the simplest layout is the printer cabled to your router, with the Pi on the same router by cable or Wi-Fi [I].
- **The plan already describes the missing Pi work.** The plan's S2-24 (SCRUM-223, To Do; `SPRINT_2_PLAN.md:4167-4300`) [V] covers:
  - the image;
  - auto-start;
  - first-boot registration with the Console's claim code;
  - reading the red button directly on the Pi.

  I propose pulling the booth part of it forward as its own ticket.
- **Until then, the internet is a single point of failure.** If your Pi loses the internet, the wheel stops, because the box runs on Render (§3 R44).

### 4.3 Third: where the QR's identity comes from, the box or the backend (a decision)

**What "identity" means on the platform [V].**
- Each QR holds one code. That code points at one record in the central database (`promo.voucher`), which says:
  - what the voucher is worth (its voucher type);
  - where and when it was made (booth, time, published wheel version);
  - who was on shift;
  - whether it is issued, used or expired.
- A box signs every record it sends with its own key, and the backend checks the signature on arrival (`apps/api/src/services/sync.ts:128`; C-B8).

| | **Design A: the box makes the code** (built; spec §11) | **Design B: the backend makes the code at each press** | **Design C: the backend gives each box a block of codes in advance** |
|---|---|---|---|
| On a press | The Pi picks the prize, makes the code, records it and prints at once. It sends the record to the backend within seconds when online: the queue is pushed every 5 s (`packages/box-agent/src/agent.ts:854`) | The Pi asks the backend over the internet to pick the prize and make the code, waits for the answer, then prints | The Pi picks the prize and takes the next code from its block |
| If the mall internet drops | The booth keeps playing and printing. Vouchers queue and go up when the line returns (spec §11) | No voucher can be printed: the wheel stops, or staff write vouchers by hand. This breaks spec §11 and the brief's rule that no device action waits on the cloud (`docs/briefs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md:175`) | The booth keeps printing until the block runs out |
| A voucher printed while offline, scanned at the park | "Code not found — the booth may not have synced yet" until the booth reconnects, then it works. [I] Families usually reach the park after the booth has reconnected | This case cannot happen: every printed code already exists | The backend knows the code but not the prize until the booth syncs, so it still has to say "not synced yet" |
| How fast a press feels | No internet involved | Every press waits on the mall connection and on Render | No internet involved |
| What exists | Built and tested (§3 R5, R12 to R15) | Not built; it replaces the built draw and code path and their offline tests | Not built; adds block top-ups, and fails when a block runs out offline |
| Risks | A booth can draw a code it has already issued: about 1 chance in 360,000 per new code after ten years at 500 spins a day, which means two or three clashes a decade per booth (`packages/shared/src/booth-code.ts:65-80`). Sync catches a clash and raises an alert, and never silently changes a code (`apps/api/src/services/sync-booth.ts:520-582`). Until staff act, the second slip scans as the first voucher [I]. The box could check its own earlier codes before printing, since the prefix belongs to that booth [I]. A Pi with a wrong clock prints wrong dates; each spin carries a flag for it (`packages/box-agent/src/booth.ts:938-958`) | A slow or dropped connection means a child waiting, or no prize at all | Block management, with no gain over A |

**Recommendation [I].** Use two designs, one for each source of vouchers.
- **Wheel vouchers use Design A.** The box makes the code, and everything the code means is defined in the backend.
- **Discount QRs the park creates itself come from the backend** (Q3). The park sets up a discount in the admin and asks for, say, 200 codes. The backend makes 200 one-time QRs, as a printable sheet or a file.
- **Both kinds share one voucher table and one redemption path.** They land in the same table and go through the same one-time redemption at the till.
- **Add the check character (§4.1, item 7).**

This gives you exactly "set up a prize in the wheel, or set up a discount, and the QRs carry an identity from the backend", without the booth stopping when the mall Wi-Fi drops.

### 4.4 Fourth: the static QRs and the old 4-digit codes

**Today, at the park's counter [V/I].**
- The counter uses two systems. First, the guest's 4-digit code is used up in Radar. Then staff scan a static QR into the old POS to take the discount off.
- That static QR is the same for every winner of the prize. Your "Spin the Wheel (100)" QR decodes to one 16-character POS voucher code (B-P20).
- Radar's campaign QRs for guests also show the campaign's shared POS code (B-P17).
- Nothing links the two scans.

**On the platform [V].**
- POS discount codes are the same kind of reusable code. Their limits are checked in the browser, and their use is counted only in the open tab (§3 R56).
- The POS admin's "downloadable QR" is a decorative pattern that no scanner can read (`apps/pos/src/lib/qr.ts:1-5, 13-33`). Do not print those for customers.

**Proposal [I].**
- From the day the new POS runs the counter, stop handing out static QRs. Every customer-facing QR becomes a one-time voucher, either made by a booth or part of a campaign batch (§4.3).
- Keep reusable codes only for staff to type, with their limits checked on the server.
- Outstanding 4-digit Radar codes (the park told customers they never expire) are imported and honoured once each. This waits on the Radar database dump (`docs/briefs/OWNER_DIRECTION.md:192`; Q6).

   - With nobody signed in, the wheel still plays and the spin is recorded as unattributed.
7. **Play.**
   - Press the red button. The box on Render picks the prize and records it, the wheel turns to it, and the card says "Take your voucher from the printer".

   - To go back to the wheel, press twice quickly (within 1.5 s), or wait 60 s.

9. **Optional checks.**
   - *The printer-failure screen.* In Virtual box 1 → Simulator, set the printer to "Paper out" and spin. The TV then shows the code and QR. Set the printer back afterwards (`apps/console/src/components/devices/SimulatorPanel.tsx:42-45`).
   - *Offline.* Press Virtual box 1 → Go offline and spin. The TV shows the offline dot and a pending count. Press Go online and it catches up (C-B12).
   - *Publishing.* Publish one prize at 100 % and watch the TV pick it up within about 90 seconds. Then publish your real list.

### 5.3 What will work, and what will not

**What will work [V]:**
- your prize list and chances, and publishing;
- pairing the Pi's screen;
- the red button, as long as it does not send Enter;
- the draw on the box and the daily limits;
- the voucher record and code;
- the wheel animation and the prize card;
- the staff PIN;
- the offline queue on the virtual box;
- the Console's live status.

**What will not work [V]:**
- **Your printer prints nothing.** The box runs on Render and cannot reach a printer on your desk (C-B9). USB printers are not supported at all.
- **The Pi is not a box.** "Add a box" in the Console gives a claim code that nothing on the Pi can use (C-B24). If the Pi loses the internet, the wheel stops.
- **The POS does nothing with the voucher.** It cannot scan, show, apply or redeem it, and nothing makes it one-time (§3 R16, R23 to R25).
- **The slip is wrong in places.** Its text is generic, the staff row always reads "Not signed in", and the terms name "HKT Central" (§3 R20).
- **Also missing:** reprint, badge sign-in, new voucher types, sound (the wheel is silent), and a booth report.

### 5.4 Please do not do these on staging during the test

- **Do not move Booth 1 to another box, or add a second booth station to Virtual box 1.** Either one makes every TV call fail with `503 BOOTH_NOT_ON_THIS_BOX`, and the TV says "Booth not ready — please call staff" (`apps/api/src/services/booth.ts:241-268`).
- **Do not "Add a box" for the Pi and move Booth 1 onto it.** It fails the same way, and nothing on the Pi can claim the box yet.
- **Do not print QRs from the POS admin's Discount codes page.** They are not real QR codes (§4.4).
- **Remember that every real spin creates a real voucher record on staging.**

## 6. Questions for the owner

These are only the questions whose answer changes the design. Each one lists the options and my recommendation.

**Q1. Where is the voucher code made?** (§4.3)
- A: on the booth box. This is built. The booth keeps printing offline, and a voucher the booth has not yet sent up reads "the booth may not have synced yet" at the counter.
- B: by the backend at each press. No voucher can be printed while the mall internet is down, and every press waits on the connection.
- C: the backend gives each box a block of codes in advance. The booth keeps printing offline, but the counter still cannot know the prize until the booth syncs.
- **Recommendation: A for the wheel, and the backend for discount QR batches (Q3).**

**Q2. When does a voucher count as used?** (§3 R27)
- A: the moment it is scanned, as with Radar today. If the sale is then abandoned, the voucher is gone.
- B: held when it is scanned, used up when the sale is paid, and released if the line is removed or the sale is voided.
- **Recommendation: B.** It matches the park's "after Reception scans and applies the prize". Also: should a scan with no sale open start one? **Recommendation: yes.**

**Q3. What does "set up a discount" cover?** (§3 R35)
- A: only discounts offered as a wheel prize.
- B: also stand-alone batches of discount QRs that the park creates in the admin, for posters, flyers, partner cards, or printing at the till. Each QR is one-time, made by the backend, and redeemed the same way as a wheel voucher.
- **Recommendation: B, straight after wheel redemption works. Only managers create batches.**

**Q4. What exactly does the POS do with each prize?** (§3 R26) Here is my reading of the park's own wording; please correct it.
- **100, 150 or 200 THB:** that amount off the ticket lines of the order, never more than the ticket total, and no change given. It cannot share a sale with another voucher or promo code ("Cannot be combined with other offers").
- **Free Bracelet Workshop:** one workshop added at no charge. Which catalogue product is it?
- **Kids Pizza:** one mini pizza and one juice of the guest's choice at no charge, at the restaurant till.
- **1+1 Kids Ticket:** with two kids tickets of the same package, one is free. Not valid for Eat & Play packages.
- **Mystery Gift (later):** staff hand over the gift, nothing goes on the bill, and the voucher is recorded as redeemed.
- **Recommendation: as listed.** Please also say whether "cannot be combined" blocks member tier prices, or only other vouchers and promo codes.

**Q5. How long is a voucher valid?** (§3 R17)
- A: an expiry per prize. This is the spec and what is built; staging uses 14 days.
- B: never, as the park's texts of 16 and 17 September say.
- C: an expiry per prize, with "never" allowed as a choice.
- **Recommendation: C, and you choose per prize.**

**Q6. What happens to the old codes at the switch?** (§4.4)
- A: outstanding 4-digit Radar codes are imported and honoured once each, and static QRs stop on the switch day.
- B: retire both on the switch day, with a notice to customers.
- **Recommendation: A.** The park told customers those codes never expire. It needs the Radar database dump.

**Q7. How do staff sign in at the booth?** (§3 R30, R31) Your kit has no keypad or scanner.
- A: keep the PIN, and add a small USB number pad at the booth.
- B: a badge scanned by a USB scanner (spec §10). This needs the badge decision first (SCRUM-218; SCRUM-200 comments).
- C: no sign-in at all, so every spin is unattributed.
- Also: should the wheel refuse to spin while nobody is signed in? Spec §5 says no, and the code agrees; SCRUM-199's comments say yes.
- **Recommendation: A now and B later. Keep spins allowed with nobody signed in.**

**Q8. Where can a voucher be redeemed?** (§3 R29)
- A: at any Oto branch, with the redeeming branch recorded.
- B: only at the branch printed on the slip.
- Kids Pizza: at the restaurant till, or at reception?
- **Recommendation: A, with the slip wording to match. Kids Pizza at the restaurant till, which then needs a scanner.**

**Q9. The bench hardware.** Your answers here change what gets built next.
- Is the printer on a network cable, or USB only? USB is not supported yet.
- Does the Pi connect by cable or by Wi-Fi?
- Will the TV stand portrait or landscape? Landscape is not supported.
- Which key does the red button send?
- **Recommendation: printer on a network cable to your router, the Pi on the same router, the TV in portrait, and the button sending Space.**

**Q10. What should the code look like?** (§3 R13)
- A: random, like `B1RT7KMQ4X`. This is built, and the TV shows it as `B1 RT7K MQ4X`. Add a check character.
- B: the sample's `LW-YYMMDD-NNNN`. It is guessable (a date plus a counter), and booths making codes offline cannot share one counter [I].
- **Recommendation: A.**

**Q11. How does a player leave the prize screen?** (§3 R6)
- A: two presses within 1.5 s, or 60 s untouched. This is built, and the old game does the same.
- B: one press and no timeout, as the park's June text says.
- **Recommendation: A for now, and decide after you try it.** Once the voucher prints, the card no longer shows a code, so one press may be enough.
