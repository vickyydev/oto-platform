# Cross-park offline wallet compatibility — 6 October 2026

The wallet identity and balance belong to one operator. A till box receives active wallets from that operator's parks as key digests, never raw band or voucher codes. Its snapshot still names the box's park and carries that park's business date and offline cap. The first 5,000 active wallets are bounded with the box's own park first; a wallet absent from that copy is refused offline. A stale copy, or one for a different park after a box is moved, is also refused. Online lookup and spending remain available under the current park's permissions.

An offline sale and its `wallet.spent` fact must still belong to the authenticated box and station at the selling park. The wallet may have been issued at another park of the same operator. Sync records the wallet tender and the spend entry at the selling park, with the existing action-key replay, row lock, cap evidence and overdraft alert. A wallet of another operator remains unavailable.

The remaining accounting choice is **per-park stored-value liability**. `walletReportOf` sums movement entries by `wallet_entry.branch_id` (where money was spent), but its live outstanding balance sums wallet rows by `wallet.branch_id` (where credit was issued). `walletLiabilityOf` is sign-exact from entries in each park; a cross-park spend makes the spending park's ledger outstanding negative while reducing the issuing park's wallet balance. Operator-wide totals still reconcile. Before claiming a per-park liability balance, decide whether liability stays with the issuing park or transfers to the spending park, and define the corresponding inter-park entries/report presentation. No transfer entry, new permission, or financial attribution rule was added here.

Root integration: API wallet 26 and cross-park replay 3, POS 14 and box 10 checks pass. API/POS/shared/box typechecks pass; changed-file lint and POS build pass. The report now explains that movement location and issuing-wallet live balance have different scopes. No inter-park transfer policy is inferred.
