# Prototype parity audit, 2 October 2026

_Why: on 2 October the owner ruled (SCRUM-488) that we follow the
prototype's existing rules and do not invent parallel ones unless
something is clearly missing or broken. The cash plan had broken that
rule. This audit checks every delivered story for the same mistake._

## How it was done

Eight read-only auditors, one per module, compared the platform code on
main (not the plans) with the prototype's code
(imports/oto-pos/artifacts/oto-till/src). A second reviewer per module
then tried to refute each finding, and only confirmed findings are
listed. A departure counts as allowed when it is one of these:

- (A) a fix to where figures come from: branch, business day, real ids,
  money as floats, data held only in memory;
- (B) something the platform needs that the prototype could not have:
  persistence, sign-in, permissions, audit, idempotency, offline boxes,
  real devices, a real payment gateway;
- (C) backed by an owner decision on record.

Everything else is listed below. Classes:

- D: a prototype rule replaced by ours without an owner ruling;
- E: a prototype rule or flow not ported;
- F: visible words, layout or flow changed.

The auditors confirmed 74 findings. Merging duplicates (the same issue
seen from two modules) leaves 65. About 130 allowed departures were
also recorded; each module's list is in the audit data, and none needs
action.

## Summary

| Group | Count | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Fix first: safety and money | 9 | Missing or changed rules that put a child's safety, a payment or stock figures at risk. Scheduled first. |
| Put back the prototype's rule | 34 | Our own preference or a missed rule, with no trade-off worth asking about. Fixed in parity rounds. |
| Decisions for the owner | 22 | Real trade-offs. Filed as one rulings ticket. Following the 2 October ruling, the prototype's behaviour goes back in unless the owner says to keep ours. |

Jira: SCRUM-489 (fix first, High), SCRUM-490 (put back: selling, payments, History, F&B, members), SCRUM-491 (put back: booking, arrival, check-in, stock), SCRUM-492 (decisions for the owner). Order: SCRUM-489 straight after End of Day round 1 lands; SCRUM-490 and SCRUM-491 after S2-15a; each SCRUM-492 item follows the prototype by default when its round runs unless the owner rules otherwise.

The pattern behind most class D findings: a plan section called
"prototype bugs not ported" or "open decisions (recommended answer holds
unless the owner objects)" that relabelled a rule choice as a bug, or
built our recommendation before the owner answered. From now on a plan
may only fix class A faults on its own authority. Every rule choice is
asked first, with the prototype as the default.

## Fix first: safety and money

### 1. Tier-proof expiry is mandatory, and an expired document silently drops the rate with no re-verify flag, so the member's sales then fail with a 409

- Module: checkout-pricing, accounts-members-offline · class D · effort M
- Prototype: components/shared/VerifyTierModal.tsx:36-58: proof type only, canConfirm = !!proofType, no expiry. Lines 70-73 say 'Saved to the member profile so they won't be asked again'. lib/membership.ts:51-55 resolveAutoTier holds the verified tier indefinitely.
- Platform: apps/pos/src/components/shared/VerifyTierModal.tsx:147-148: expiryValid is required for canConfirm, while line 207 keeps the 'won't be asked again' text and its own comment at 82 says 'a document with no expiry never does'. apps/api/src/routes/members.ts:490 requires evidenceExpiresAt. members.ts:130-142 hides an expired verification, so the till's resolveAutoTier (apps/pos/src/lib/membership.ts:51-55) falls back to the default tier. Nothing resets member.tier_code on expiry: tierCode is written only at members.ts:541 and member-tier.ts:171, and no review flag or expiry job exists. sale.ts:526-534 prices from m.tierCode. The till sends lineTotalSatang at the default price (apps/pos/src/api/sales.ts:844) for the discounted member tier, so sale.ts:1485-1497 throws 409 SALE_LINE_PRICE_MISMATCH. This was traced by reading the code, not driven on staging.
- Effect: Every verification now lapses. Once it lapses, that member's ticket sales fail at quote or Pay with an unexplained price-mismatch error until a document is re-checked. The visible dialog promises the opposite.
- Action: Revert to the prototype: a verification does not lapse. Keep the expiry field optional (the brief allows that). If an expiry date is recorded, flag the member for re-verification instead of dropping the rate, and make the till and the server read the same tier.

The client brief says the same: POS_BACKEND_LOGIC.md:189 'The gate reader checks this flag only'; reconciliation R-53 and R-83 (POS_RULES_RECONCILIATION.md:108, :148) and C5 '`gateAccess` on adult bands only'.
