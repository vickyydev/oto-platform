# OTO Park — the decisions only you can make

14 October 2026

The 13 October list had 116 discussion points. Most of those were technical
choices, and we have now settled them ourselves and recorded them — you do
not need to read them. This document keeps only what is genuinely yours to
decide: money rules, what staff are allowed to do, a few customer-facing
policies, numbers to confirm, and some information only you can supply.

**How to answer:** reply with the number and either "agree" or your
alternative. Every question carries our recommendation, so "agree to all"
is a complete answer. Nothing here is urgent enough to block daily work;
items marked ● unblock a piece of waiting work the moment they are answered.

## A. Money rules

**A1. ● A party's money on the daily report.** When a family pays today for
a party happening next month, should today's revenue include that payment,
or should the money count on the party's date?
*Our recommendation: count it on the party's date (today shows only payments
for parties held today).* This decides what "today's party revenue" means on
your dashboard.

**A2. ● Refunding prepaid lunch money.** Today, refunding a child's prepaid
lunch leaves the lunch credit still spendable — a family could get the money
back and still spend the credit.
*Our recommendation: the refund removes the unspent credit, and a refund is
refused once the credit has already been spent.*

**A3. ● Credit vouchers without payment.** The till currently lets reception
issue vouchers that add spendable credit nobody paid for.
*Our recommendation: only managers may issue these, and they appear under
promotions in the reports so given-away credit is always visible.*

**A4. Credit spent at the other park.** When credit bought at Floresta is
spent at Chalong, whose books carry it?
*Our recommendation: the selling park keeps the money and the liability; the
spending park's report shows the redemption as a separate line, so network
totals always add up.*

**A5. Camp walk-ups.** When a child joins a camp partway through, should the
desk charge once for the remaining days, or charge again at each day's
check-in?
*Our recommendation: charge one day's price once, and the child is registered
from today to the end.*

**A6. A sale that was never finished (confirm).** We built this already with
a sensible rule and want your nod: when a sale took some money but never
completed (for example card declined after a deposit), a manager refunds
everything taken and the sale files under "cancelled" in the records, with
day-end figures balancing automatically.
*Keep it this way unless you prefer something else.*

**A7. Money during an internet outage (two linked rules).**
(a) If the card terminal took the payment but the till never got the answer,
staff may record the terminal's approval code so the sale is captured and
reconciled later. (b) Wristband credit spent offline is capped at **฿300 per
wristband per day**, and same-day credit expires that night.
*Our recommendation: yes to both; the ฿300 cap is adjustable at any time.*

**A8. Opening stock.** Count opening stock separately for each storage place
(kitchen, shop, store room), not once for the whole park.
*Our recommendation: per place — this is expensive to change later, so we
want your explicit yes.*

**A9. Different VAT by category.** Does any branch ever need a different tax
treatment per product category (for example food taxed differently from
tickets)? Today everything uses one branch rule and an unused
category-exception option sits in the system.
*Our recommendation: if you never need this, we retire the unused option.*

## C. Customers and families

**C1. Extra play time and child care.** When a family buys extra play time,
should a child's supervised-care (drop-off/nanny) time extend with it, or is
care time booked and paid separately?
*Our recommendation: keep them separate — care staff plan around firm end
times.*

**C2. Party extras and the kitchen.** Should party add-ons (extra pizzas,
socks) create normal kitchen tickets, stock movements and sales records like
any other order, or stay only on the party bill?
*Our recommendation: create the normal records — the kitchen sees the order
and stock stays true.*

**C3. Discounts on printed receipts.** Printed paper receipts currently show
no discount lines for any sale — a family that used a promo code sees only
the final total on paper (the screen shows the detail).
*Our recommendation: add the discount line to printed receipts.*

**C4. Old families in the new member list.** When we import the old system's
data, should past check-in families automatically become member records
(marked unverified), giving reception a full list from day one?
*Our recommendation: yes, import them as unverified members.*

**C5. How long we keep records and photos.** Propose: child visit photos —
30 days after release; financial and child-release records — 5 years
(legal); ordinary operational records — 2 years. Chat/conversation media
will follow the same schedule when the Inbox is built.
*Confirm or adjust these periods — nothing is deleted until you approve the
schedule.*

**C6. Face clock-in for staff.** It stays switched off (staff use PIN/phone).
Turning it on in production later needs a planned re-enrollment of everyone's
face and your explicit go, since it is biometric data.
*Our recommendation: keep it off until you decide otherwise. Nothing to do
now unless you want it scheduled.*

## D. One number that touches everything

**D1. The business day starts at 05:00.** Reports, prices, promotions and
daily staff benefits all reset at 05:00 Bangkok time, not midnight — so late
trading stays with the same day's cash-up and a promotion does not flip at
midnight mid-shift.
*Confirm 05:00 as the single boundary for all of these.*
