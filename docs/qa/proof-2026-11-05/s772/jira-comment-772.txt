Checked on staging (the test system) on 5 November 2026, 02:06 to 02:16 Bangkok, on the live version d630b22c (the till, the Console and the platform were all on it). Only test records were used (names start with ZZ TEST), cash only, on a test till made for this check and removed afterwards.

What a person can do now:
- When a customer should get the Thai rate but cannot show an ID, staff pick Thai and press "Special case, approve by hand" under the list of documents.
- They pick one of three reasons (Thai child with a foreign parent; Thai ID seen earlier, not to hand; Other) and can add a note. No PIN and no manager are needed: the till records who approved it and when.
- The sale gets the Thai price for that sale only (2 Hours Play: 520 baht for the child instead of 890). The member stays unverified, so on the next visit the till asks for proof again.
- The sale and History show who approved it and why, for example "Thai rate approved by hand by Som: Thai child with a foreign parent — (the note)", on the till and on the phone till.
- "Other" needs at least two words and refuses a number. Closing the box changes nothing. Expat has no such button: it is for the Thai rate only.
- The normal document check (for example Passport) works as before and is still saved to the member.
- In the Console, Watch > Activity shows each approval with who did it, the reason and the note, and Catalogue & money > Tier verifications counts the approvals by hand per staff member.

How to retest:
- On the staging till, sign in as reception and look up a test member (or make one whose name starts with ZZ TEST).
- Pick Thai. In "Verify Thai rate", press "Special case, approve by hand", pick a reason, add a note if you like, and press "Approve Thai rate by hand".
- Add a ticket: it shows the Thai price. Pay by cash.
- Open History and the sale: the line says who approved it by hand and why.
- Look the same member up again: it still says "No verified rate", and Thai asks for proof again.
- Try Other with one word, then with a number in it: both are refused.
- In the Console, open Tier verifications: your name shows with the count.

Not shown on staging: the message on a till that has lost the platform. No test till on staging was offline, and making one would take every staging till offline for everyone; that message is covered by the automatic checks instead.
